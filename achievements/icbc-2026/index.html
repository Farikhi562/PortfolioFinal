International Canvas Business Competition 2026

A Universitas Gunadarma team led by Muhamad Fauzan Al Farikhi reached the final and finished in the Top 4 (Juara Harapan 1) at ICBC 2026, presenting the NEXA-Sphere business concept.

What happened

ICBC 2026 is an international business competition organized by Universitas Dian Nuswantoro. The final presentation took place on April 25, 2026 under the theme “Digital Business Ecosystem for Global Market Expansion.” Our team presented in front of the judges with the countdown timer running, and finished in the Top 4 with the title Juara Harapan 1.

What we presented

The team presented NEXA-Sphere, a B2B AI-SaaS concept that turns point-of-sale history into purchasing recommendations for multi-outlet F&B businesses, built around demand forecasting, procurement recommendations with owner approval, and a centralized dashboard. Read the full project write-up on the NEXA-Sphere project page or the article on the road to ICBC.

My role

Team Lead and presenter for NEXA Tech Labs. On the technical side I directed the technology architecture, developed the predictive-model (demand forecasting) approach, and handled algorithm risk management.
