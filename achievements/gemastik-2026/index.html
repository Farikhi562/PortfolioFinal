About GEMASTIK

GEMASTIK (Pagelaran Mahasiswa Nasional Bidang Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi) is a national student competition in information and communication technology in Indonesia. Universities send delegations chosen through internal selection.

My delegation

Universitas Gunadarma announced its delegation for GEMASTIK 2026 after the third wave of internal selection, and I am listed as a main delegate in the ICT Business Development division. The division fits the business and product side of my work at NEXA Tech Labs, alongside my projects such as NEXA-Sphere.

Official record

The delegation is listed in the official announcement from Kemahasiswaan Universitas Gunadarma: Pengumuman Penetapan Delegasi Universitas Gunadarma pada GEMASTIK Tahun 2026 (Hasil Seleksi Internal Gelombang III).
