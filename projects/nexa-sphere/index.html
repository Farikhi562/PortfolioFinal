NEXA-Sphere

Prototype

A B2B AI-SaaS platform concept for optimizing inventory and supply-chain operations of multi-outlet F&B businesses, built around demand forecasting, procurement recommendations, and a centralized dashboard.

Problem

Multi-outlet F&B SMEs (2–10 branches) are too small for enterprise ERP yet too complex for manual, instinct-based purchasing — leading to overstock, stockout, and food waste.

Solution

A B2B AI-SaaS platform that turns historical point-of-sale data into purchasing recommendations: demand forecasting, procurement recommendations with owner approval, and a centralized multi-outlet dashboard.

My role

Team Lead & AI/ML Engineer

Contribution focus

Technology architecture direction, predictive-model (demand forecasting) development, and algorithm risk management; presented the proposal at ICBC 2026 as team lead.

Technology stack

  • Time-series forecasting (ARIMA, Prophet) — proposed
  • Gradient boosting (XGBoost / LightGBM) — proposed
  • Cloud (AWS / GCP) — planned

Current status

Business-proposal concept with interface prototypes — finalist (Top 4, Juara Harapan 1) at ICBC 2026. Not released and not yet in wide use. Screenshots in the proposal use simulated internal data.

Supports skills
