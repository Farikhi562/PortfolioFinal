The 2026 courses behind my AI engineering work, with dates: Python and data science at Gunadarma, plus Dicoding courses on AWS Cloud, Gen AI and Kiro.

Skills are easier to trust when they come with dates and certificates. This is the learning path behind my AI engineering and software work in 2026, in order.

February 2026: Python fundamentals

Dasar Pemrograman Python (Fundamental of Python) at Universitas Gunadarma, February 23–28, 2026. The course covered building web applications with Flask and MySQL, including CRUD.

May–June 2026: data science

Pengantar Ilmu Data (Fundamental of Data Science) at Universitas Gunadarma, May 25 – June 6, 2026. The material covered matrices, working with data in Python, NumPy and Pandas, and the data science methodology.

October 2026: Dicoding courses

The Dicoding certificates are verifiable online; see the certificate list on the achievements page.
